# PoultryPro™

### Smart Farming. Better Decisions. Better Profits.

PoultryPro™ is a cloud-based, AI-powered poultry farm management and farm intelligence platform developed by **Greenfield Contracts & Agro Limited**.

It helps poultry farmers digitize farm operations, monitor performance, identify risks, analyse trends, forecast outcomes, and make better-informed farm management decisions.

PoultryPro is designed for small and medium-scale farmers as well as larger commercial poultry operations.

# 🧠 AI Farm Intelligence

PoultryPro is being developed beyond conventional digital farm record keeping into an intelligent poultry farm decision-support platform.

The AI Farm Intelligence layer is designed to analyse validated farm data, identify unusual patterns, forecast possible outcomes, and provide explainable recommendations to farmers.

### Current Intelligence Capabilities

- Farm-specific performance baselines
- Data quality validation
- Anomaly detection
- Trend detection
- Production forecasting
- Mortality-risk analysis
- Feed analysis
- Financial and profitability forecasting
- Farm Health Score
- Explainable AI insights
- AI Farm Assistant
- AI recommendations
- Recommendation approval
- Recommendation rejection
- Recommendation dismissal
- Farmer feedback
- Recommendation outcomes
- Learning signals
- Anonymised peer benchmarking
- Recommendation history and audit tracking

# 🔄 Controlled AI Learning

PoultryPro is designed around a controlled learning architecture rather than blindly training on every piece of information entered by farmers.

The intended intelligence cycle is:

```text
Farm Data
    ↓
Data Validation
    ↓
Analysis
    ↓
Pattern & Anomaly Detection
    ↓
Prediction
    ↓
AI Recommendation
    ↓
Farmer Review
    ↓
Approve / Reject / Dismiss
    ↓
Action & Outcome
    ↓
Learning Signal
    ↓
Improved Future Intelligence
